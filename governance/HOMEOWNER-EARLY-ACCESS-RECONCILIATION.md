## Conflicts and precedence

1. **Public CTA label:** the experience says existing “Request service” entry points *lead* to early access. MDS §9 and its R0 addendum reserve that commitment label for admitted homeowners. Use truthful **Join early access** wording on public recruiting actions; retain **Request a service** only for a scoped admitted account. Destination and selected-service context follow the experience.
2. **Fields and consent authority:** the source calls minimum fields, out-of-area capture and marketing consent proposed or open. DEC-2026-022 / CFG-014 and the MPS R0 addendum subsequently govern these: email, Lee County ZIP and service interest or “still exploring”; optional first name; separate expansion-interest path; independent unchecked marketing choice; withdrawal and retention as recorded in the layered-launch decision. Exact privacy copy and technical handling still require R0.2 acceptance before live collection.
3. **Closed checkout and existing records:** the source's direct-URL closed state could be read as blocking all checkout recovery for a non-invited account. The MPS/MTS R0 addenda and R0.1 preserve legitimate existing transactions and history. Apply admission to **new** request/checkout authorization while keeping valid prior transaction and recovery paths accessible; verify these cases at the backend and UI.
4. **MDS approval wording:** source §6 asks for composition/copy approval before implementation, while its final paragraph records Josh's 2026-09-28 approval. Treat the composition and stated copy as approved direction. The rendered route/state sweep and accessibility evidence remain R0.3 gates.

No other conflict with the current MDS or R0 roadmap was found. The source's `/early-access` path is a proposal accepted as the R0.3 route target here, subject to normal route implementation review. This record does not change Phase 6's order or accept any R0 slice. The companion Credits and Founding Vendor source artifacts remain missing under the layered-launch decision and retain their later gates.
