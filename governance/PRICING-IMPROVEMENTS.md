## 2. Design

**Identity (PR-01).** `vendor_packages.id` remains the identity every request, quote and history row references. `lineage_id` is the stable package identity; a replacement revision is a new row in the lineage, and publishing pauses the previous revision in the same transaction. At most one active row per lineage (unique partial index). A trigger keeps a lineage within one vendor and service and makes it immutable. Every existing row is its own lineage. The revision fingerprint is the SHA-256 of the package content document.

**One calculation (PR-05).** `private.price_selection(content, cadence, tier, answers, add-ons)` prices a content document in whole cents. The same function prices live packages (`private.package_content`) in request preview and submission, operator drafts and unsaved edits (`pricing_preview_content`), and vendor previews (`pricing_preview_draft`). Its status is `exact` only when one price level applies and every component has one fixed price; otherwise `estimate`, `quote`, `unresolved` or `invalid`, with a reason. Submission stores the result, the package revision and lineage in `service_requests.pricing_inputs` (previously unused) so later catalog edits never change it; a stale `package_revision` is refused as `price_changed`.

**Price meaning (PR-02).** Fixed packages carry exact prices only (trigger-enforced); starting-at and range prices live on quote packages, so an estimate is never a payable total. A fixed selection that becomes an estimate (a range add-on) is submitted as a quote request with the estimate kept. Legacy tiers keep `price_kind` null and their current meaning; nothing is backfilled or reclassified.

**Add-ons (PR-03).** Unit label, quantity bounds, exact or range price, required or optional, cadences. Required add-ons are always included and cannot be deselected; quantities outside bounds, add-ons for another cadence and add-ons that charge for included scope are refused. Extra-can one-time and recurring rates are two explicit add-ons, never inferred from text.

**Rules (PR-04).** A level matches when its legacy rule and every `rule_conditions` entry hold (inclusive bounds). Exactly one matching level gives an exact price; none or several give no exact price; the finder (also used by matching) no longer picks the cheapest of overlapping levels. Authored content is refused when two rule-based levels of a cadence can match the same home, when only some levels of a cadence have rules, or when a rule names a question the package does not ask as a number. Levels without rules stay homeowner-chosen options; without a choice the request keeps today's behavior (the lowest level, its scope shown).

**Approval (PR-07–PR-09).** Operator drafts hold the full content. States: draft → sent → approved → published, with changes requested back to the operator. The fingerprint covers content, vendor, service and target package; any change returns the draft to draft (re-saving the same content keeps an approval; restoring earlier content does not restore it). Only the authenticated account bound to the provider with the vendor role can approve or ask for changes, for the revision sent; operators cannot approve on a vendor's behalf; publishing needs an operator, a reason and the approved revision. Retries are idempotent. Sends are in-app notifications; the operator sees not sent / in inbox / read / removed and "no email sent". An unbound vendor blocks the send with the reason, recorded. History records saves (with changed fields), sends, blocks, approvals, change requests, publications and discards. The vendor view omits operator identity.

**Promotions (PR-10).** See DEC-2026-038 and `20261018003000`. Offers have one funder, a revision fingerprint, scope, dates with the campaign timezone recorded, limits, budget (Mercurius funding), optional code (hash) and first-booking condition. Vendor funding is live only after the bound vendor approves the revision; Mercurius funding only after an operator authorizes it with a reason. Reservations are evaluated from trusted records, one per request, under a lock on the offer, idempotent by key, and snapshot the rate, base, discount, funding, subsidy and revision. Releasing a finalized redemption is refused. Nothing is wired into submission, checkout, refunds or payout.

**Offer application (DEC-2026-042, TRACE-116, `20261018006000`).** On the final review, each exactly priced service lists the live public offers it qualifies for, with the server's saving on that service and its add-ons; "No offer" is the starting choice and the greatest saving is labelled "Best savings" without being selected; a code finds code-only offers (10 unknown codes per homeowner per hour). Submission reserves the chosen offer in the same transaction (`promotion_reserve` under the offer lock) and refuses the plan (`promotion_changed`) when the saving or revision differs from what was shown. `private.promotion_selections` records the homeowner's choice; until checkout starts the homeowner can replace or remove the offer from the request, and `money_source` refuses a request whose offer was released or whose confirmed price changed. A request with add-ons or an offer is matched only to the package, level and revision it was priced from, at the total including add-ons; recurring visits keep the priced add-ons; add-ons without a chosen offering are refused. Cleanup (`run_promotion_release_sweep`) releases unpaid reservations whose terms expired or whose offer ended or changed, exactly once, under checkout's locks; scheduling is prepared, not active ([PRICING-ROLLOUT.md](PRICING-ROLLOUT.md) §6). Retried in-app approval sends reuse an unread notice. All of it stays behind the closed gate pending the finance review ([PRICING-FINANCE-REVIEW.md](PRICING-FINANCE-REVIEW.md)).

**Owner decisions (DEC-2026-041, TRACE-115).**
- *Add-on checkout.* `money_source` prices a request with add-ons again from the package content and requires the same exact total from the same package revision; the snapshot's add-on amount and discount must equal the source; publication stays two-person (`money_publish_snapshot` actor + approver). The intake offers optional add-ons for an offering the homeowner chose.
- *Commission and subsidy.* `private.money_split(obligation, retained service)` returns `round(service × 15 / 100)` and no subsidy for an ordinary snapshot (bit-for-bit the reviewed formula), and the snapshot's commission and subsidy scaled by the retained share for a promotional one. Every fee site (earnings, refund, refund reversal, chargeback loss, payable, proceeds, ACH, reconciliation) uses it; provider proceeds = retained service + subsidy − fee + tip; the subsidy posts to `promotion_subsidy`. So a refund reverses vendor proceeds, commission and subsidy proportionally, and a vendor-funded discount (not cash) adds no refund entitlement.
- *Redemptions.* First booking = no completed Marketplace service; one active first-booking redemption per homeowner (unique index); revalidated in `money_source` until the homeowner commits (first checkout attempt); committed at full capture; released when the unpaid request is cancelled or its terms expire unpaid (sweep); restored after a full refund before service with nothing retained; otherwise only by `admin_restore_promotion` with a reason. Promotions stay closed at checkout behind `money_feature_gates.promotions_checkout`.
- *Corrections.* `admin_correct_package_prices` refuses; `admin_draft_from_package` copies the live package (legacy rules become conditions; legacy quote levels become "starting at", reported) into a replacement draft, optionally with corrected prices and a reason; `admin_suspend_package` pauses a listing with a reason. `admin_package_contents` reports approval evidence as recorded: vendor approved, operator published, managed template, or unrecorded (no authorship is recorded for vendor-editor rows, so no approval is claimed).
- *Unlinked vendor.* With no bound account and a contact email on file, a send queues one email notice per revision; a server route claims it with the service key, sends through Resend (no reply-to, no price) and records sent/failed/unknown with redacted errors; operators see the state and can resend. Email is never approval.
- *Unmatched answers.* `price_selection` reports the blocking answers (or the combination) and no amounts once answers match no level; preview and submission return them; the intake shows "Price requires confirmation" with the answer.

## 8. Rollout and rollback

The current runbook, including `20261018006000`, the cleanup schedule, the gate and the replacement drafts, is [PRICING-ROLLOUT.md](PRICING-ROLLOUT.md); the paragraph below is the 2026-10-19 summary.


Additive migrations; hosted push needs the owner's authorization (dry run first), before merge (DEC-2026-037). Existing packages keep their rows, prices and meaning; lineage is backfilled to each row's own id; drafts are backfilled to content and stay unapproved. Request preview, submission, the eligibility finder and the checkout source are restated; their behavior is unchanged for packages without add-ons, rule conditions or overlapping rules, which is every live package today. Migrations `20261018004000` and `20261018005000` (TRACE-115) are additive too: new tables, columns, functions, a trigger, two unique indexes (same-name active packages; one active first-booking redemption; both empty on hosted per §4), the `promotion_subsidy` account, and a widened `money_ach_items` balance check (equal to the old one when the subsidy is 0). They restate reviewed money functions; for every snapshot without a promotion the results are identical (full SQL suite unchanged). The promotions gate starts closed. The hosted push needs the owner's dry run first; the `20261018005000` money restatements need the two-person finance review before the gate opens. Rollback: promote the previous Vercel deployment; the schema can stay (the previous app ignores the new columns and functions). A database rollback would be a forward fix restoring the previous function bodies from `20261016002000`, `20260928000000` and `20260905010000`.
