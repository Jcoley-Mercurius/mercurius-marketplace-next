# PRODUCT.md — Mercurius

## Platform
Mercurius is a managed home-services marketplace for Southwest Florida (Cape Coral, Fort Myers, Lee County and nearby). It connects homeowners, HOAs, and property managers with vetted local service providers through package pricing, request flows, photo-documented job completion, and platform coordination.

This is a **managed marketplace**, not an open free-for-all directory. Mercurius coordinates matching, scheduling support, and job visibility so homeowners do less chasing and vendors do less lead-hunting.

## Users
- **Homeowners / snowbirds / property managers** — need reliable local services with clear pricing when available, simple requests, updates, and photo proof.
- **Service providers (vendors)** — need platform-sourced work, package pricing tools, less admin, and a professional local channel.
- **Admins / operators** — approve vendors, review applications, and manage operational queues.

## Product purpose
Help local homeowners get home services handled with less friction, and help local pros get clearer work without constant lead chasing — through a calm, trustworthy digital platform.

## Positioning
- Local-first (SWFL), not national scale theater
- Managed coordination over pure self-serve marketplace chaos
- Clear package pricing when a provider has published rates
- Honest about quote/matching services and limited early coverage
- Practical and reliable, not flashy AI SaaS

## Soft-launch constraints (binding)
Mercurius is in soft launch / early vendor growth. Agents and UI must obey these rules:

1. **No fabricated scale** — do not invent active vendor counts, jobs completed, dollars paid, ratings aggregates, or review volume.
2. **No fake social proof** — no invented customer or vendor names presented as real reviews.
3. **Availability honesty** — live fixed pricing only when backed by active provider packages; otherwise show quote/matching or “not available yet / request to source.”
4. **Support honesty** — do not claim every vendor gets a dedicated account manager on day one unless that operating model is truly staffed.
5. **Coverage honesty** — limited local network is fine to acknowledge; never imply universal service coverage.
6. **Role clarity** — homeowners book/request; vendors apply and run jobs; admins operate the back office.

## Brand commitments
- Trustworthy, calm, capable
- Professional without corporate coldness
- Warm without folksy
- Intelligent without generic AI aesthetics
- Florida / local credibility over abstract tech theater

## Design principles for agents
- Prefer clarity of the next action (request, apply, review job, approve)
- Marketing surfaces can be more open and persuasive; product surfaces favor speed and legibility
- Keep Reimagine™ / AI features grounded and useful, never sci-fi
- When in doubt, choose the calmer, clearer, more honest option
- Soft-launch pages emphasize credibility and truth over visual experimentation

## Accessibility
- WCAG AA minimum; aim higher on critical text and CTAs
- Never rely on color alone for status
- Comfortable touch targets on primary actions
- Clear focus states

## Evidence on hand
- Working Next.js app with homeowner, vendor, and admin portals
- Live Supabase backend (auth, roles, applications, jobs, packages, contact submissions)
- Soft-launch vendor growth focus in Southwest Florida
- Real product flows: request service, vendor apply, package pricing, job status, photo completion

## Anti-references
- Generic purple-to-blue SaaS gradients
- Fake metrics dashboards
- Nested card spam
- “AI magic” visual language
- National marketplace claims
